GUEST DATA PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy — HOTEL Vivana

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Siripuram, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 530003

1. Commitment to Guest Privacy

HOTEL Vivana ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us during room reservations, Rasa Kitchen dining inquiries, and website visits.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal details essential for fulfilling your stay and guest services, including:

  • Full Name, Contact Phone Number, and Email Address.
  • Government-issued photo identification details during check-in (mandated by local Visakhapatnam police regulations).
  • Check-in and Check-out dates, room type preferences, and special dietary requests.
  • Payment information processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

3. Security & 256-Bit SSL Encryption

All online reservation interactions on our website are transmitted using industry-standard 256-Bit SSL encryption. Payment transactions adhere strictly to PCI-DSS standards, ensuring your credit card and financial data are never stored in plaintext on our servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to confirm reservations, provide front-desk hospitality (e.g., room readiness, concierge assistance with Mr. Murali), issue invoices, and communicate direct discount offers if opted-in.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure smooth navigation and remember room preferences during multi-step reservation booking. We do not sell or rent guest data to third-party advertisers.
