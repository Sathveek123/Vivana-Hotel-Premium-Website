# HOTEL Vivana - Luxury Boutique Hospitality Website

> **"Where the City Meets the Coast"** — Siripuram, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

HOTEL Vivana is an elevated 3-star boutique luxury hotel website built with a modern, responsive HTML5/CSS3 design system, dynamic hero video background, authentic Andhra culinary storytelling, and transparent guest review integration.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Highlights

- **Hero Video Background**: High-definition video loop (`img/vivana_hero.mp4`) with smooth, responsive `object-fit: cover` alignment across all mobile, tablet, desktop, and ultrawide viewports.
- **Integrated Live Tide & Weather Ribbon**: Real-time coastal Vizag information (sunrise, sunset, and optimal Ramakrishna Beach walk hours).
- **Multi-Page Site Architecture**:
  - `index.html`: Master landing page featuring awards bar, social proof counters, "Ask Murali" local concierge section, and populated 4-category review dashboard.
  - `rooms.html`: Room inventory (Deluxe, Premium, Executive, Family, Presidential Suite), price anchoring, scarcity badges, and side-by-side comparison matrix.
  - `dining.html`: The Rasa Recipe Archive (Grandmother's Konaseema Mutton Pulao & Natu Kodi Pulusu stories).
  - `events.html`: Shubham Banquet Hall landing, wedding packages, & inquiry form.
  - `offers.html`: Promotional packages & `VIVANA10` discount code.
  - `about.html`: Heritage story, staff spotlights, Radical Honesty section, & repeat guest stories.
  - `contact.html`: Siripuram location block, phone, WhatsApp link, & contact form.
  - `terms.html` & `cancellation.html`: Legal & privacy compliance policies.
- **Synthesized 4.2★ Review Dashboard**: 3,034+ verified Google & TripAdvisor reviews categorized into *All*, *Mutton Pulao & Dining*, *Staff Hospitality*, and *Room Comfort*.
- **Native Touch-Swipe Carousels**: Optimized horizontal scroll snapping (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`) for smooth app-like swiping on mobile devices.
- **Performance Optimized**: Sub-second load times, preloaded assets, `loading="lazy"`, and `decoding="async"`.
