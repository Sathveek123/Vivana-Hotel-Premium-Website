Cancellation & Refund Policy — HOTEL Vivana

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Siripuram, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 530003

1. Flexible Rate Cancellations

Reservations made under Flexible Rate terms can be cancelled with zero penalty up to 24 hours prior to standard 12:00 PM check-in time.

2. Non-Refundable / Promotional Rates

Bookings made under non-refundable promotional packages are non-cancellable, but dates can be modified up to 48 hours prior to arrival subject to room availability.

3. Refund Processing Time

Eligible refunds for cancelled reservations are processed back to original payment mode within 5-7 business banking days.
